What is DASP?

Your Super Doesn't Have to Stay in Australia

If you worked in Australia on a temporary visa, your employer was required to pay superannuation contributions into a super fund on your behalf. When you leave Australia permanently, you're entitled to claim that money back.

This is called a Departing Australia Superannuation Payment (DASP) — and it can be worth thousands of dollars. But the process involves dealing with the ATO, multiple super funds, and a significant amount of paperwork.

Most people don't know where to start — or lose out entirely. That's where Reacco comes in.

Who can claim?

Anyone who worked in Australia on a temporary visa (including Working Holiday, Student, or Skilled visas) and has permanently departed the country.

How much can I get back?

It depends on how long you worked and your salary, but claims can range from a few hundred to many thousands of dollars. Every dollar counts.

Is there a time limit?

There's no strict deadline, but don't wait too long — unclaimed super balances are eventually transferred to the ATO's lost members register.

Can I claim from overseas?

Absolutely. Reacco works with clients all over the world. You don't need to be in Australia — we handle everything remotely.
